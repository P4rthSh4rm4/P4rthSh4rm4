## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. SKILLSETU — Skills & Career Ecosystem

**Role:** Team Leader · **Team:** Bytecodes · **Context:** Smart India Hackathon

SKILLSETU is a unified platform concept designed to bring **students, academicians, industry, and institutions** into one ecosystem. The goal is to connect skill development and career opportunities while supporting stronger collaboration between learners, educational institutions, and employers.

**Problem we are addressing**
- Student skills and industry requirements can be difficult to align.
- Career, mentoring, internship, and institutional workflows may be spread across separate systems.
- Institutions need useful feedback about changing industry skill requirements.

**Capabilities being explored in the project**
- 🎯 **AI-based skill and career matching:** Map skills and potential skill gaps to relevant learning and career opportunities.
- 🧩 **Skill-based talent discovery:** Help employers discover candidates based on relevant skills.
- 📈 **Role match score:** Estimate how closely a learner's skills align with a role's requirements.
- 📄 **Resume analysis:** Explore resume-to-role alignment and improvement suggestions.
- 🏫 **Four connected portals:** Student, Academician, Industry, and Institution.
- 🤝 **Collaboration workflows:** Support areas such as mentoring, internships, projects, research, consultancy, and faculty development.

**My role as Team Leader**
- Helping coordinate the team's work and communication.
- Contributing to the translation of the problem statement into features and a prototype.
- Collaborating across project, data, and model-related tasks.

**Technology note:** Supabase has been considered for the backend. The exact implementation status of individual features may vary; planned or explored capabilities should not be interpreted as fully deployed features.

### 2. Loan Prediction Model

A machine-learning project focused on predicting a loan approval outcome from applicant-related data.

**Workflow**
1. Understand the dataset and target variable.
2. Inspect and prepare the data, including missing values and categorical features where applicable.
3. Explore relevant features and split the data for training and evaluation.
4. Train a suitable classification model.
5. Evaluate performance with appropriate metrics and review prediction errors.
6. Communicate the model's limitations alongside its results.

**Skills demonstrated through this project**
- Data preprocessing and feature preparation
- Supervised classification workflow
- Model training and evaluation
- Applying machine learning to a practical problem

### 3. Resume ATS & Placement Helper

A project focused on helping job seekers review how well a resume aligns with a target job description and identify areas to improve.

**Intended workflow**
1. Provide a resume and target job description.
2. Extract and compare relevant skills, keywords, and qualifications.
3. Highlight potential gaps or missing role-related terms.
4. Suggest ways to improve clarity and role relevance.
5. Present feedback as guidance—not as a guarantee of interview selection or a substitute for recruiter judgment.

**Areas explored**
- Resume and job-description matching
- Text processing and skill/keyword extraction
- AI-assisted career support
- Turning analysis into clear, actionable feedback
